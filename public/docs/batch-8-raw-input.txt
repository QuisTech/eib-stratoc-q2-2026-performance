Slide 1:
PRESENTED BY

COMPREHENSIVE 90-DAY PLAN FOR THE PROCUREMENT DEPARTMENT OF EIB GROUP

Slide 2:
OBJECTIVE: The objective in the next 90-days is to establish an efficient, transparent, cost-saving, and technology-driven procurement function for the EIB Group.

Slide 3:
STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE: The strategic objective over the period is to:
Implement a centralized procurement process for all subsidiaries.
Reduce procurement costs through competitive sourcing and vendor management.
Develop and deploy procurement and inventory management tools.
Strengthen inventory control and warehouse management.
Enhance staff capability through training and development.
Improve governance, compliance, and financial oversight.

Slide 4:
PHASE ONE: DAYS 1 – 30 (FOUNDATION AND ASSESSMENT)

Slide 5:
1.Establishment of procurement governance structure and reporting lines: 
This entails defining the rules that govern how the organization purchase items. The idea is to have a clear framework that dictates who carries out the procurement function. The set-out plan is to have a situation where the various subsidiaries and departments generate a check list of the items they need while the purchase of those items will be handled by the procurement department.

Slide 6:
2. Review existing procurement processes and requisition workflows: 
Establishing a robust and efficient template for the procurement process is central to achieving optimal performance in procurement. The plan is to meet with staff and relevant stakeholders in the organization’s procurement chain to review the existing procurement process with a view to making fundamental changes that will make it a seamless process. The target is to streamline the process to ensure that all procurement activities are carried out professionally and only through the procurement department.

Slide 7:
3. Work with IT to redesign the requisition portal and include procurement approval stages:
Given that the requisition at EIB group is generated online and the fact that the present design of the requisition portal does not have provision for the procurement manager’s input makes it imperative that the portal be redesigned to reflect the new process where the procurement manager is responsible for raising requisitions. Discussions have been held with the IT department in this regard and they are already working on it.
 
4. Identify frequently purchased items and critical operational requirements:
An inventory of the items in the store will be done to ascertain the present level of stock with the aim of stocking up much of the frequently purchased items and other items that are critical to our operations.

Slide 8:
5. Engage and prequalify at least three vendors per major procurement category:
This will be done with the intention of establishing a large vendor base or pull from where we can pick at any time there is procurement to be made. The process of identifying potential vendors for the organization has started with the searchlight on vendors supplying items needed in key areas of our operations, including construction materials, IT equipment, foodstuff, home appliances, electronics, electrical equipment, Power and energy solutions, office equipment and stationery.
 
6. Assess warehouse/store requirements:
A comprehensive assessment of the warehouse and store will be done in collaboration with the facilities department to ascertain what need to be fixed or provided to enable it function as a standard facility for the safe keeping of the company’s properties or assets. This is very important if we must ensure accountability and prevent losses due to theft or spoilage especially with respect to food items.

Slide 9:
7. Complete onboarding of redeployed staff and inventory manager:
The three staff posted to work in the procurement department are from the PGA department of Luft Pay Tv. An inventory officer was also employed to join the team and they are all gradually settling down and beginning to embrace the challenges of the assignment. The lack of a separate office for the procurement department is a challenge for us as we are forced to operate from the Luft paytv office for the time been. The plan is to have a procurement office that will accommodate the team as it will greatly help our delivery on the job. We hope this will happen soon.
 
8. Enroll procurement personnel in Procurement and supply-chain training:
The need to equip the staff with the requisite skills needed for the job is paramount in our plans given that some of us have little or no experience in procurement. To this end, we are all taking various online courses on procurement and supply management. With time I intend to liaise with the training department to have a procurement professional come train the staff on the rudiments of the job.

Slide 10:
PHASE 2: DAYS 31 – 60 (IMPLEMENTATION AND OPTIMIZATION)

Slide 11:
1 Launch revised procurement procedures and approval workflows:
The implementation of the outcome of the stakeholders meeting on improving on the existing procurement process, will begin immediately with the approval by management and its adoption as the new or revised procurement procedure. Recognizing the fact that all requisitions are to be handled by the procurement department is key to the successful implementation of the process.
 
2. Begin pilot testing of procurement and inventory management software:
The software been developed by the IT department will be tested to see how far it helps to make the procurement process and inventory management better. The pilot test is expected to last for a week and will involve only a few subsidiaries and departments.

Slide 12:
3. Create approved vendor database and vendor performance scorecards:
After reaching out to potential vendors in all areas of the organization’s operations, a meeting Will be held with them following which a comprehensive database of all the approved vendors Will be compiled and put in a database to serve as reference whenever procurement is to be made. Also, guidelines for evaluating the performance of the vendors will be established 
 
4. Conduct market surveys and benchmark pricing
The success of the procurement process will be measured by how well the company is able to purchase good quality products at low prices. This will help to lower the cost of operations and central to achieving this is to carry out regular market research. In this way we will be able to bargain effectively with vendors to ensure the best for the organization.

Slide 13:
5. Implement inventory tracking and stock-taking procedures:
Procedures for tracking inventory will be implemented and it will entail daily stock-taking by
the inventory officer with adequate documentation. The software developed by the IT department will be deployed to ensure effective inventory tracking.

Slide 14:
PHASE 3: DAYS 61 – 90 (CONSOLIDATION AND PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT)

Slide 15:
1 Deploy procurement software organization-wide:
After the successful testing of the procurement software developed by the IT department it will be deployed for use across the organization. It means that we will now have be a standard central procurement process for the organization which all subsidiaries are expected to key into. 

2. Stock essential and frequently requested items in the approved store:
Using the inventory of what is available in the warehouse and the check list of needed items gotten from the various subsidiaries, we will purchase and stock the store with the essential and frequently used items for our operations. This will ensure that the items are readily available when needed,  help to curb some of the irregularities associated with urgent or emergency purchase and also help to reduce the effect of any sudden increase in the prices of some of these items that may obstruct our operations.

Slide 16:
3. Evaluate supplier performance and renegotiate pricing where necessary:
The performance of our vendors or suppliers will be assessed based on how well they have been able to meet up with supply request. Guarantee of fair price, swift response to supply demands and adherence to organization’s procurement conditions are some of the indices on which such evaluation will be based. The process will help identify the performers and non-performers which will aid our decision making going forward.
 
4. Conduct procurement compliance audit:
An organization wide procurement compliance audit will be conducted to ascertain  how well the procurement software is working and the level of adherence to the standard central procurement process. Such evaluation will help determine if the software is truly effective in making our procurement process seamless and smooth which is strategic to the growth of the organization.

Slide 17:
5. Measure cost savings achieved during the first 90 days:
Fundamental to the work of the procurement department is to ensure a reduction in the operational cost of the organization. Every cost saving measure taken in the course of procurement without compromising on quality is therefore a welcome development. Measuring achievement recorded in this regard will be a good way of accessing how well the procurement process is working.
