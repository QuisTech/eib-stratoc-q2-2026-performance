DETAILED PROGRESS REPORT ON THE ACTIVITIES OF BRIECH UAS



Reporting Period: January – May 2026



1. INTRODUCTION

This report presents a comprehensive overview of the activities, achievements, operational support engagements, technical developments, training exercises, financial utilization, and challenges encountered by Briech Unmanned Aerial Systems (Briech UAS) during the reporting period.

Briech UAS has continued to make significant progress in strengthening indigenous unmanned aerial capabilities through strategic drone design and development, direct operational support to security agencies, specialized technical training, research and innovation, and internal capacity expansion. During the period under review, the department successfully executed several critical projects and operational tasks aimed at enhancing national security support and advancing local UAV technology.

The achievements recorded demonstrate Briech UAS’s commitment to becoming a leading indigenous provider of advanced unmanned aerial solutions for surveillance, tactical operations, and defense applications.



2. PROJECT ACHIEVEMENTS, OPERATIONAL ACTIVITIES, AND TECHNICAL MILESTONES

2.1 Completion of the Design of Multiple Briech UAS Drone Variants

One of the key achievements during the reporting period was the successful completion of the conceptual, engineering, and technical designs of multiple drone variants under the Briech UAS development program.

These drone variants were designed to support a range of mission requirements, including:

Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR) 

Tactical battlefield support 

Long-endurance aerial monitoring 

Payload delivery operations 

Specialized combat support missions 

Significant design improvements focused on:

Airframe Optimization

Enhanced aerodynamic efficiency, structural durability, and weight management to improve overall flight performance.

Mission Flexibility

Development of modular systems allowing quick adaptation of drones to different operational roles and payload configurations.

Indigenous Capability Development

Reduction in dependence on imported drone solutions through local design and engineering innovation.

The completion of these designs represents a critical milestone toward self-sufficiency in indigenous UAV production.





2.2 Operational Support to the Nigerian Army in Shiroro LGA, Niger State

Operational Period: 17 February – 3 March 2026

Briech UAS successfully deployed personnel and drone assets in support of the Nigerian Army during military operations in Shiroro Local Government Area, Niger State.

The mission focused on providing aerial intelligence and surveillance support to improve operational effectiveness.

Key Contributions

Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR)

Drone systems were deployed to conduct:

Wide-area aerial surveillance 

Monitoring of suspected hostile activities 

Terrain reconnaissance in inaccessible locations 

Detection and tracking of suspicious movements 

Real-Time Operational Awareness

Live drone feeds provided operational commanders with:

Immediate situational updates 

Enhanced decision-making capability 

Improved coordination of troop movement 

Reduced exposure of personnel to ambush risks 

Tactical Planning Support

Aerial imagery and mapping data were used to support route planning and operational targeting.

The deployment significantly enhanced the effectiveness of military operations and demonstrated the operational value of UAV integration in modern security operations.



2.3 Conduct of Kamikaze Drone Training for Nigerian Army Personnel

Briech UAS successfully conducted specialized technical training for selected Nigerian Army personnel on the deployment and operational handling of FPV Kamikaze Drone Systems.

The training program was designed to transfer critical operational knowledge and build indigenous tactical drone deployment capability within the armed forces.

Training Areas Covered

Introduction to FPV kamikaze drone systems 

System assembly and configuration 

Flight control and maneuvering techniques 

Target acquisition and precision engagement 

Mission planning and operational safety 

Tactical deployment strategies 

Maintenance and troubleshooting procedures 

Post-mission assessment and reporting 

Training Impact

The training improved:

Personnel operational competence 

Tactical readiness 

Independent handling capability 

Institutional UAV knowledge within the Nigerian Army 

This activity marks an important contribution by Briech UAS to national defense capability development.



2.4 Operational Support to Zamfara State Government

Briech UAS also provided aerial operational support to the Zamfara State Government in addressing ongoing security challenges within the state.

Activities included:

Drone-based surveillance missions 

Monitoring of vulnerable locations 

Forest corridor reconnaissance

The support strengthened situational awareness and improved early threat detection capabilities.



2.5 Signing of Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with DICON

A major strategic milestone achieved during the reporting period was the successful signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Briech UAS and Defence Industries Corporation of Nigeria (DICON).

The partnership is intended to promote collaboration in:

Indigenous drone technology development 

Defense systems innovation 

Technical knowledge sharing 

Local manufacturing and production capacity expansion 

Joint research and development initiatives 

The MoU establishes an important framework for strengthening Nigeria’s local defense technology ecosystem and advancing strategic self-reliance.



2.6 Design and Development of Briech UAS Ground Control Station (GCS)

Substantial progress was recorded in the design and development of the Briech UAS Ground Control Station (GCS).

The indigenous control system is being developed to provide centralized command and control for UAV operations, including:

Mission planning 

Flight monitoring 

Telemetry tracking 

Payload management 

Real-time video integration 

Data logging and post-mission analysis 

This development is expected to reduce dependence on foreign software and improve operational autonomy.



2.7  Design and Development of Quality Management System (QMS) Software

During the reporting period, Briech UAS initiated and made significant progress in the design and development of an indigenous Quality Management System (QMS) Software aimed at strengthening internal quality processes, improving product reliability, and enhancing operational accountability across all technical and production activities.

The software is being developed as an integrated digital platform to support quality control, quality assurance, documentation management, and data trend analysis across drone design, manufacturing, assembly, testing, and maintenance operations.

Key Functional Areas of the QMS Software

Quality Control Management

The system is designed to improve inspection and validation processes by enabling:

Recording and tracking of component inspections 

Monitoring of product conformity against technical specifications 

Identification and reporting of defects and non-conformities 

Inspection checklist automation 

Corrective action tracking and resolution management 

This will help ensure that all drone systems and related components meet established technical and operational standards before deployment.



Quality Assurance Processes

The software will support structured quality assurance activities through:

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) compliance monitoring 

Process validation and workflow approvals 

Audit planning and internal audit record management 

Continuous improvement tracking 

Preventive action documentation 

This functionality is intended to strengthen consistency, accountability, and compliance across all Briech UAS operational units.



Documentation and Records Management

A central feature of the QMS software is the establishment of a secure digital documentation system for managing critical technical records.

This includes:

Design documentation control 

Version tracking for engineering drawings and technical manuals 

Maintenance and servicing records 

Test reports and certification records 

Operational mission documentation 

Training records and technical competency logs 

The digital documentation platform will improve information accessibility, traceability, and record security.



Data Trend Analysis and Performance Monitoring

The software incorporates analytical capabilities for monitoring quality trends and operational performance over time.

This includes:

Failure trend analysis 

Defect rate monitoring 

Maintenance frequency analysis 

Component reliability tracking 

Production performance indicators 

Automated dashboards and reporting tools 

These analytical tools will support evidence-based decision-making and enable proactive identification of recurring technical issues.



Strategic Importance of the QMS Software

The development of this software represents a major step toward institutionalizing a culture of quality within Briech UAS and aligning operational practices with internationally recognized quality management principles.

Expected benefits include:

Improved product reliability and consistency 

Faster identification and correction of defects 

Enhanced traceability and accountability 

Better decision-making through data-driven insights 

Strengthened readiness for future certification and regulatory compliance 

Increased operational efficiency across engineering and production teams 

The successful implementation of this Quality Management System will significantly enhance Briech UAS’s internal control environment and support its long-term objective of becoming a world-class indigenous unmanned aerial systems organization.

CONCLUSION

The Second Quarter (March–June 2026) represents one of the most productive and strategically significant periods in the growth and development of Briech UAS Limited. During the reporting period, the company successfully executed multiple high-impact projects across training, production, operations, research and development, regulatory compliance, maintenance support, and business development while maintaining a strong commitment to safety, quality, and operational excellence.



Notable achievements during the quarter included the successful training of sixteen (16) Nigerian Army personnel on FPV drone operations, production and deployment of sixty (60) FPV drone systems for operational use, recurrent training and proficiency enhancement of newly recruited and existing pilots, development of NCAA-compliant operational manuals and certification documentation, and the successful coordination of reconnaissance activities at the Ngaski Mining Site to support potential commercial engagements.



In addition to operational deliverables, all technical departments actively participated in research and development initiatives aimed at addressing emerging operational challenges and improving the company's technological capabilities. Significant progress was recorded in drone design and manufacturing, payload systems enhancement, communications technology, software development, automation systems, artificial intelligence applications, inventory management systems, and advanced FPV technologies. These initiatives continue to strengthen Briech UAS' position as an indigenous technology-driven unmanned aviation company capable of developing innovative solutions tailored to local operational requirements.

The company also maintained a robust maintenance and technical support programme for drones deployed across operational camps, ensuring fleet serviceability and mission readiness. Through continuous engagement with pilots, regular safety awareness campaigns, recurrent Crew Resource Management (CRM) training, Human Factors education, and proactive operational oversight, Briech UAS successfully achieved an outstanding safety record during the reporting period.
