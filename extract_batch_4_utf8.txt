Bright FM 90-Day Action Plan  

Purpose: This document outlines Bright FM’s key priorities, actionable milestones, and expected outcomes for the next 90 days. The objective is to provide with a clear framework to track progress, measure impact, and support continuous improvement.

The focus of this plan is not only on activities but on measurable progress ensuring that every effort contributes towards building a stronger radio brand, improving audience connection, strengthening content quality, and creating a stronger foundation for commercial growth.

90-Day Strategic Focus

Over the next quarter, Bright FM will focus on four key areas:
1. Strengthening content quality and programme identity.
2. Improving audience engagement and brand visibility.
3. Building stronger commercial opportunities through better programme packaging.
4. Improving internal structure, accountability, and performance tracking.

Strengthening the Team to Support Bright FM’s Growth

As Bright FM begins this next phase of growth, it is important to understand that achieving the goals in the 90-day action plan will require more than just good ideas and programmes. It will require the right people with the right skills to help bring those plans to life.

Radio is a business built around people. The presenters, producers, and marketing team are the ones who create the connection between the station, listeners, and advertisers. A strong station is not only about having airtime; it is about having people who understand how to create engaging content, build relationships, and grow the brand.



While the current team has continued to support the operations of the station, there is a need to strengthen the team with additional experienced hands who can help Bright FM achieve its growth targets.

To support this direction, Bright FM will require:

1. Two (2) Radio Marketers

The addition of dedicated radio marketers will help the station become more active commercially.

Their responsibilities will include:

* Reaching out to potential advertisers and business partners.

* Creating relationships with organisations that can work with Bright FM.

* Finding sponsorship opportunities for programmes and segments.

* Helping the station become more visible in the market.

* Supporting efforts to increase revenue.

At the moment the station requires more than waiting for adverts to come in. It requires people who actively go into the market, present the value of the station, and build long term relationships with brands.

2. Two (2) Experienced Radio Presenters

The addition of experienced presenters will help improve the quality and identity of Bright FM’s programmes.

They will support:

* Better programme delivery.

* More engaging conversations with listeners.

* Stronger storytelling.

* Development of fresh programme ideas.

* Improving the overall sound and professionalism of the station.

Experienced radio professionals will also help transfer knowledge and improve the confidence and skills of existing team members.

Why This Is Important

The goals set for the next 90 days  improving content, growing the audience, increasing visibility, and creating more commercial opportunities can only happen when the station has the right support structure.

This is not just about adding more staff. It is about bringing in the skills needed to help Bright FM grow into a stronger and more competitive radio brand.

Investing in the right people will help Bright FM create better programmes, connect more deeply with listeners, attract advertisers, and build a stronger future.



 1. Content Improvement & Programme Strengthening

Actions: Review existing programmes, improve segment structure, strengthen storytelling, and encourage better programme preparation.

Timeline: Month 1-3

Measurement: Improved programme consistency, better content planning, listener feedback, and stronger programme identity.

2. Audience Growth & Engagement

Actions: Increase audience interaction, promote programmes more effectively, and create more relatable content based on listener interests.

Timeline: Month 1-3

Measurement: Increased listener participation, improved social media engagement, and stronger audience connection.

3. Programme Branding & Commercial Packaging

Actions: Identify programmes with sponsorship potential and develop clearer value propositions for advertisers.

Timeline: Month 1-3

Measurement: Number of sponsorship proposals developed, advertiser engagement, and commercial discussions initiated.

4. Digital Presence Improvement

Actions: Extend radio content beyond traditional broadcasting through online content sharing and audience engagement.

Timeline: Month 1 - 3

Measurement: Increased digital visibility, content consistency online, and improved online audience interaction.

5. Internal Performance Improvement

Actions: Improve coordination between programming, news, engineering, and management teams through clearer responsibilities and reporting.

Timeline: Month 1 - 3

Measurement: Better workflow, timely reporting, improved accountability, and stronger teamwork.

Executive Summary
Six subsidiaries assessed for structure, workflows, and optimization. Key recommendations:
Standardize Procedures
Unified operating procedures across subsidiaries
Automate & Digitize
Adopt digital workflow systems and ERP
Decentralize Authority
Delegate decision-making to reduce bottlenecks
Strengthen Performance
Implement KPIs and management dashboards
Cross-Subsidiary Synergy
Enhance integration and collaboration

SECTION 1
Bright FM — Broadcasting
Structure
GM → DGM (Programming Unit, News Dept) + Engineering Dept. Lean hierarchy with minimal bureaucracy.
7-Step Workflow
Planning → Content Development → Pre-Broadcast Coordination → Broadcast → Issue Escalation → Commercial Activities → Performance Review → Continuous Improvement
Key Gaps
No Commercial Unit → Revenue constraints
Limited Digital Media Team → Limited audience reach
High GM dependence → Delayed decisions
Recommended: Establish Sales & Marketing, Digital Media Unit, and delegate authority.
