EIB STRATOC LTD

90-DAY STRATEGIC OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE PLAN

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

EIB Stratoc Ltd has established itself as one of Nigeria's leading indigenous Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance (ISR), and security technology companies, delivering integrated surveillance, intelligence support, drone operations, and camp security management services across multiple operational locations. Through the deployment of Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), PTZ and CCTV surveillance systems, Fusion Centres, and dedicated field personnel, the company continues to support government institutions, security agencies, mining operations, and other strategic partners in safeguarding critical assets and enhancing situational awareness.

As EIB Stratoc expands its operational footprint from seventeen (17) active camps with additional deployments planned, the need for greater operational coordination, standardized reporting, stronger accountability, and improved communication between headquarters, Fusion Centres, and field locations has become increasingly important. While the company has built a solid operational foundation, sustaining growth will require continuous refinement of operational processes, investment in personnel capability, and improved utilization of existing technology and intelligence resources.

This 90-Day Strategic Operational Excellence Plan provides a practical roadmap for strengthening EIB Stratoc's operational effectiveness over the next three months. Rather than introducing an entirely new direction, the plan builds upon initiatives already underway while introducing additional measures that enhance operational consistency, decision-making, service delivery, and organizational readiness. It is designed to reinforce existing strengths, address operational gaps, and establish systems that will support the company's continued expansion.

A major priority during this implementation period will be the continued standardization of operational reporting. The recently established Report Review and Analysis (RRA) Team has commenced implementation of a revised reporting framework aimed at improving the quality, consistency, and analytical value of reports received from Fusion Centres and operational camps. Over the implementation period, the RRA Team will continue to guide adoption of the standardized reporting templates, conduct quality reviews, provide feedback to operational personnel, and strengthen management's ability to make timely decisions based on accurate and actionable operational information.

The plan also recognizes that technology alone cannot guarantee operational success. Continued investment in personnel development remains essential. Responsibility-based and technical refresher sessions that have already commenced will continue throughout the implementation period to improve surveillance monitoring, incident reporting, communication protocols, operational discipline, and collaboration between Fusion Analysts and field personnel. Particular emphasis will be placed on strengthening communication between Fusion Centres and camps to ensure timely dissemination of information, coordinated incident response, and improved operational effectiveness.

Recognizing existing operational realities, the strategy also seeks to improve coordination in areas affected by network instability, delays in responding to operational and welfare requests, and the rapid deployment of newly recruited personnel. While some of these challenges depend on Group-level approvals and resource allocation, EIB Stratoc will establish internal monitoring mechanisms, prioritization procedures, and structured escalation processes to ensure that operational challenges are documented, communicated promptly, and managed more effectively pending resolution.

Implementation of this strategy will be driven through three progressive phases. The first phase will focus on strengthening operational governance, validating current capabilities, reinforcing reporting standards, and establishing measurable performance baselines. The second phase will concentrate on optimizing surveillance operations, enhancing intelligence coordination, strengthening communication between operational units, and embedding performance management into daily operations. The final phase will consolidate these improvements by institutionalizing best practices, evaluating operational performance, strengthening stakeholder confidence, and preparing the organization for sustained growth and future expansion.

At the end of the ninety-day implementation period, EIB Stratoc is expected to demonstrate measurable improvements in reporting quality, operational coordination, surveillance effectiveness, personnel readiness, camp support, and management oversight. Executive Management will benefit from improved operational visibility through standardized reporting, structured performance reviews, and enhanced communication between all levels of the organization. More importantly, the initiatives implemented during this period will establish a stronger operational framework capable of supporting the company's expanding responsibilities while reinforcing its reputation as a trusted provider of intelligence-driven security solutions.



PHASE I (DAYS 1–30)

Operational Assessment, Reporting Consolidation and Performance Alignment

The first thirty days of this Strategic Operational Excellence Plan will focus on consolidating existing operational improvement initiatives while establishing stronger governance, standardized processes, and measurable performance indicators across all EIB Stratoc operations. As the company continues to support surveillance and security operations across multiple locations, maintaining consistency in operational execution requires continuous assessment of personnel, technology, reporting systems, and management practices.

A comprehensive operational assessment will be conducted across all Fusion Centres and operational camps to establish an accurate baseline of current performance. The review will examine surveillance operations, personnel deployment, equipment serviceability, communication infrastructure, reporting compliance, shift management, welfare support mechanisms, and adherence to Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). Particular attention will be given to identifying recurring operational challenges affecting service delivery, including communication gaps between Fusion Analysts and field personnel, network reliability, reporting inconsistencies, and response timelines for operational support requests. The findings will provide Executive Management with a clearer understanding of current strengths, existing constraints, and priority areas for improvement.

During this phase, significant emphasis will be placed on strengthening operational reporting. Building upon the ongoing work of the Report Review and Analysis (RRA) Team, implementation of the standardized reporting framework will continue across all operational locations. Fusion Analysts, Team Leads, Camp Supervisors, and other reporting personnel will be guided on the proper use of the approved reporting templates, while periodic report reviews will be conducted to improve accuracy, consistency, clarity, and analytical value. Feedback from the RRA Team will be used to address common reporting deficiencies and reinforce compliance with established reporting standards. By the end of this phase, management should observe greater uniformity in Daily Situation Reports (SITREPs), Incident Reports, Weekly Operational Reports, and Executive Briefs.

Operational governance and accountability will also be strengthened through the introduction of structured performance monitoring. Clear operational expectations will be communicated to all Team Leads, Camp Supervisors, Fusion Analysts, and camp personnel. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) will be established to measure reporting compliance, surveillance feed availability, incident response timelines, shift handover compliance, equipment readiness, and overall operational performance. Weekly operational review meetings will provide an opportunity to assess progress, identify emerging challenges, and implement corrective actions where necessary.

Recognizing that personnel remain the organization's most valuable resource, the first thirty days will also reinforce ongoing technical and operational development initiatives. Responsibility-based and technical refresher sessions already introduced within the organization will continue, focusing on surveillance monitoring procedures, incident documentation, communication protocols, operational discipline, and effective coordination between Fusion Centres and operational camps. For newly deployed personnel, emphasis will be placed on ensuring that minimum operational orientation requirements are completed before deployment wherever operational circumstances permit, thereby improving readiness while balancing the urgency of operational commitments.

To strengthen communication and collaboration across the organization, structured engagements will be held with Team Leads, Camp Managers, Camp Supervisors, and other operational personnel to review current operational practices, discuss recurring challenges, and identify practical improvement opportunities. This collaborative approach will encourage ownership of the implementation process while ensuring that recommendations are informed by operational realities across all deployment locations.

By the conclusion of the first thirty days, EIB Stratoc will have strengthened its operational baseline through improved reporting standards, enhanced governance, clearer performance expectations, reinforced communication structures, and a more comprehensive understanding of operational challenges. These achievements will provide the foundation for optimizing surveillance operations, intelligence coordination, and organizational performance during the subsequent phases of the strategy.



PHASE II (DAYS 31–60)

Operational Integration, Process Optimization and Performance Enhancement

Having established a clear operational baseline during the first phase, the second thirty-day implementation period will focus on strengthening operational integration, improving surveillance effectiveness, enhancing communication between operational units, and embedding a culture of accountability and continuous improvement across EIB Stratoc's operations.

A major priority during this phase will be the optimization of Fusion Centre operations. As the primary command and monitoring hubs for surveillance activities, the Main Fusion Centre, Cindy Fusion Centre, and Plateau Special Activity Centre (PSAC) play a critical role in maintaining situational awareness across all operational camps. Efforts will therefore be directed towards improving coordination between Fusion Analysts and field personnel through clearly defined communication channels, standardized operational procedures, and structured shift handover processes. Team Leads will be expected to conduct routine operational briefings at the commencement and conclusion of each shift to ensure continuity of operations, effective information transfer, and prompt identification of operational concerns requiring escalation.

Building upon the progress made through the Report Review and Analysis (RRA) Team, report quality assurance activities will become a routine operational function during this phase. Weekly report review sessions will be conducted to assess the quality of Daily Situation Reports (SITREPs), Incident Reports, Intelligence Briefs, and Weekly Operational Reports submitted from all operational locations. Feedback provided through these reviews will focus on improving analytical depth, consistency, factual accuracy, and the relevance of operational recommendations. The objective is not only to improve documentation but also to ensure that reports support timely decision-making and contribute meaningfully to operational planning.

Personnel development efforts will continue through structured technical and operational engagements designed to reinforce responsibility, professionalism, and operational competence. Rather than introducing extensive classroom-based training, emphasis will be placed on short, practical learning sessions integrated into routine operations. These engagements will focus on surveillance monitoring techniques, effective communication between Fusion Analysts and camp personnel, incident classification, report writing, radio communication protocols, and operational decision-making. Team Leads and Camp Supervisors will also receive guidance on leadership responsibilities, performance supervision, and effective coordination of operational activities within their respective areas of responsibility.

Operational communication between headquarters and field camps will receive increased attention during this implementation period. While communication systems have generally supported operational activities, recurring network instability and delays in transmitting information have occasionally affected surveillance effectiveness and operational responsiveness. To better manage these challenges, an Operational Communications Register will be introduced to document recurring communication disruptions, network outages, surveillance feed interruptions, and equipment-related issues across all operational locations. This register will provide management with reliable operational data to support timely escalation to Group Management while enabling interim mitigation measures at the operational level.

Recognizing that timely support to field personnel directly influences operational effectiveness, the company will also strengthen the monitoring of operational requests and welfare-related issues originating from the camps. A centralized Request Tracking Register will be established within the Office of the Deputy General Manager to record requests requiring management intervention, monitor progress, and improve follow-up with relevant departments. Although financial approvals and major logistical support remain subject to Group-level processes, improved tracking and prioritization will enable EIB Stratoc to communicate more effectively with stakeholders while ensuring that urgent operational requirements receive appropriate attention.

Performance management will become increasingly data-driven during this phase. Operational Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) established during Phase I will be monitored through monthly performance reviews involving the General Manager, Deputy General Manager, Camp Manager, Human Resource Officer, Team Leads, and other relevant operational personnel. These reviews will assess reporting compliance, incident response timelines, surveillance feed availability, shift attendance, operational discipline, equipment functionality, and camp performance. Trends identified during these reviews will inform management decisions, operational adjustments, and resource allocation.

To support the company's planned expansion into additional operational locations, an organizational readiness assessment will also be undertaken during this period. Existing supervisory structures, staffing levels, reporting lines, equipment availability, and operational support systems will be reviewed to determine their capacity to support new camp deployments. Recommendations arising from this assessment will guide future recruitment, supervisory arrangements, infrastructure requirements, and resource planning while ensuring that operational quality is maintained as the organization grows.

By the end of Phase II, EIB Stratoc is expected to have strengthened operational coordination between headquarters and field locations, significantly improved the quality and consistency of operational reporting, reinforced communication between Fusion Centres and camp personnel, established practical mechanisms for monitoring operational requests, and embedded a stronger culture of accountability across all levels of the organization. These improvements will position the company to focus on long-term sustainability, performance optimization, and institutionalization of best practices during the final implementation phase.

PHASE III (DAYS 61–90)

Institutionalization, Performance Evaluation and Strategic Readiness

The final phase of the 90-Day Strategic Operational Excellence Plan will focus on consolidating the improvements implemented during the previous phases while establishing systems that promote continuous operational excellence, accountability, and organizational resilience. The emphasis during this period will be on evaluating progress, institutionalizing best practices, strengthening management oversight, and positioning EIB Stratoc for sustainable operational growth.

A comprehensive operational performance review will be undertaken to assess the effectiveness of all initiatives implemented throughout the ninety-day period. The review will evaluate improvements in surveillance operations, reporting quality, communication effectiveness, personnel performance, operational coordination, and overall service delivery. Data collected through performance reviews, operational assessments, the Report Review and Analysis (RRA) Team, and management engagements will be analysed to determine areas of significant improvement as well as operational gaps requiring continued attention.

The standardized reporting framework introduced during the implementation period will be formally institutionalized as the official reporting standard across all Fusion Centres and operational camps. Compliance reviews will be conducted to ensure consistent utilization of approved reporting templates and adherence to reporting timelines. Lessons learned during implementation will be incorporated into the reporting process to improve report quality, analytical value, and management decision-making. The RRA Team will continue to function as a quality assurance mechanism by conducting periodic report reviews, providing constructive feedback, and recommending improvements where necessary.

Operational performance management will be further strengthened through structured performance evaluations of Team Leads, Camp Supervisors, Fusion Analysts, and other operational personnel. Performance discussions will focus on operational discipline, communication effectiveness, reporting quality, incident management, teamwork, and adherence to Standard Operating Procedures. Rather than serving as a disciplinary exercise, these evaluations will identify opportunities for coaching, mentorship, recognition of outstanding performance, and targeted capacity development.

Recognizing that sustainable operational excellence requires continuous learning, the organization will establish a structured operational knowledge-sharing framework. Weekly operational debriefs, incident review sessions, and lessons-learned discussions will be incorporated into routine management activities to encourage continuous improvement and promote the sharing of best practices across operational locations. Significant operational incidents and successful response strategies will be documented to strengthen institutional knowledge and improve future operational planning.

As EIB Stratoc continues to expand its operational footprint, particular attention will be given to organizational readiness for additional deployments. Findings from the expansion readiness assessment conducted during Phase II will be translated into practical recommendations covering manpower planning, supervisory capacity, operational infrastructure, communication systems, equipment requirements, and support services. These recommendations will support Executive Management in making informed decisions regarding future deployments while ensuring that organizational growth does not compromise operational quality or service delivery.

To further strengthen operational resilience, an Operational Improvement Register will be established within the Office of the Deputy General Manager. This register will capture recommendations arising from management reviews, operational inspections, staff engagements, report reviews, and field observations. Each recommendation will be assigned responsible officers, implementation timelines, and progress indicators to ensure continuous monitoring beyond the initial ninety-day implementation period. This mechanism will reinforce a culture of accountability while ensuring that operational improvements remain an ongoing organizational priority rather than a one-time exercise.

The organization will also conclude the implementation period with an Executive Operational Review involving the General Manager, Deputy General Manager, Camp Manager, Human Resource Officer, Team Leads, and other key operational stakeholders. The review will assess achievements recorded during the implementation period, evaluate progress against established Key Performance Indicators, identify outstanding operational challenges, and develop recommendations for the next phase of organizational improvement. The outcome of this review will serve as the foundation for future operational planning and strategic decision-making.

By the conclusion of the ninety-day implementation period, EIB Stratoc is expected to have established stronger operational governance, improved surveillance coordination, enhanced reporting standards, strengthened communication across all operational levels, improved personnel accountability, and reinforced management oversight. More importantly, the organization will have developed practical systems capable of sustaining continuous improvement while supporting future operational expansion and strengthening its reputation as a provider of professional intelligence-driven security solutions.

IMPLEMENTATION GOVERNANCE

Successful implementation of this Strategic Operational Excellence Plan requires active participation from all operational units and sustained oversight by Executive Management. While strategic direction will be provided by the General Manager, day-to-day coordination and implementation will be led by the Office of the Deputy General Manager in collaboration with the Camp Manager, Human Resource Officer, Team Leads, Camp Supervisors, and the Report Review and Analysis (RRA) Team.

The Deputy General Manager will coordinate implementation activities, monitor progress against planned initiatives, facilitate interdepartmental collaboration, and provide periodic implementation updates to the General Manager. Team Leads and Camp Supervisors will be responsible for driving implementation within their respective operational areas, ensuring compliance with approved procedures, and providing timely feedback on operational challenges and achievements.

Monthly implementation review meetings will be held to assess progress, evaluate emerging operational risks, review Key Performance Indicators, and agree on corrective actions where necessary. This governance framework is intended to ensure that implementation remains structured, measurable, and aligned with the organization's strategic objectives.

Effective implementation of this Strategic Operational Excellence Plan requires coordinated leadership, accountability, and collaboration across all operational units of EIB Stratoc Ltd. The successful delivery of the initiatives outlined in this document will depend on the active participation of management, supervisory personnel, Fusion Centres, and operational camps.

Overall strategic oversight shall be provided by the General Manager, while the Deputy General Manager shall coordinate implementation, monitor progress, facilitate collaboration among operational units, and provide periodic progress reports to Executive Management.

The Camp Manager shall oversee the execution of field-related operational initiatives, ensuring that Camp Supervisors, Team Leads, and operational personnel comply with established procedures and performance expectations.

Weekly operational review meetings.

Monthly management performance review meetings.

Quarterly Executive Operational Review.

Periodic camp inspections and operational assessments.

Continuous monitoring of agreed Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

While communication systems have generally supported operational activities, recurring network instability and delays in transmitting information have occasionally affected surveillance effectiveness and operational responsiveness. To better manage these challenges, an Operational Communications Register will be introduced to document recurring communication disruptions, network outages, surveillance feed interruptions, and equipment-related issues across all operational locations. This register will provide management with reliable operational data to support timely escalation to Group Management while enabling interim mitigation measures at the operational level.

Recognizing that timely support to field personnel directly influences operational effectiveness, the company will also strengthen the monitoring of operational requests and welfare-related issues originating from the camps. A centralized Request Tracking Register will be established within the Office of the Deputy General Manager to record requests requiring management intervention, monitor progress, and improve follow-up with relevant departments. Although financial approvals and major logistical support remain subject to Group-level processes, improved tracking and prioritization will enable EIB Stratoc to communicate more effectively with stakeholders while ensuring that urgent operational requirements receive appropriate attention.

Performance management will become increasingly data-driven during this phase. Operational Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) established during Phase I will be monitored through monthly performance reviews involving the General Manager, Deputy General Manager, Camp Manager, Human Resource Officer, Team Leads, and other relevant operational personnel. These reviews will assess reporting compliance, incident response timelines, surveillance feed availability, shift attendance, operational discipline, equipment functionality, and camp performance. Trends identified during these reviews will inform management decisions, operational adjustments, and resource allocation.

To support the company's planned expansion into additional operational locations, an organizational readiness assessment will also be undertaken during this period. Existing supervisory structures, staffing levels, reporting lines, equipment availability, and operational support systems will be reviewed to determine their capacity to support new camp deployments. Recommendations arising from this assessment will guide future recruitment, supervisory arrangements, infrastructure requirements, and resource planning while ensuring that operational quality is maintained as the organization grows.

By the end of Phase II, EIB Stratoc is expected to have strengthened operational coordination between headquarters and field locations, significantly improved the quality and consistency of operational reporting, reinforced communication between Fusion Centres and camp personnel, established practical mechanisms for monitoring operational requests, and embedded a stronger culture of accountability across all levels of the organization. These improvements will position the company to focus on long-term sustainability, performance optimization, and institutionalization of best practices during the final implementation phase.

PHASE III (DAYS 61–90)

Institutionalization, Performance Evaluation and Strategic Readiness

The final phase of the 90-Day Strategic Operational Excellence Plan will focus on consolidating the improvements implemented during the previous phases while establishing systems that promote continuous operational excellence, accountability, and organizational resilience. The emphasis during this period will be on evaluating progress, institutionalizing best practices, strengthening management oversight, and positioning EIB Stratoc for sustainable operational growth.

A comprehensive operational performance review will be undertaken to assess the effectiveness of all initiatives implemented throughout the ninety-day period. The review will evaluate improvements in surveillance operations, reporting quality, communication effectiveness, personnel performance, operational coordination, and overall service delivery. Data collected through performance reviews, operational assessments, the Report Review and Analysis (RRA) Team, and management engagements will be analysed to determine areas of significant improvement as well as operational gaps requiring continued attention.

The standardized reporting framework introduced during the implementation period will be formally institutionalized as the official reporting standard across all Fusion Centres and operational camps. Compliance reviews will be conducted to ensure consistent utilization of approved reporting templates and adherence to reporting timelines. Lessons learned during implementation will be incorporated into the reporting process to improve report quality, analytical value, and management decision-making. The RRA Team will continue to function as a quality assurance mechanism by conducting periodic report reviews, providing constructive feedback, and recommending improvements where necessary.

Operational performance management will be further strengthened through structured performance evaluations of Team Leads, Camp Supervisors, Fusion Analysts, and other operational personnel. Performance discussions will focus on operational discipline, communication effectiveness, reporting quality, incident management, teamwork, and adherence to Standard Operating Procedures. Rather than serving as a disciplinary exercise, these evaluations will identify opportunities for coaching, mentorship, recognition of outstanding performance, and targeted capacity development.

Recognizing that sustainable operational excellence requires continuous learning, the organization will establish a structured operational knowledge-sharing framework. Weekly operational debriefs, incident review sessions, and lessons-learned discussions will be incorporated into routine management activities to encourage continuous improvement and promote the sharing of best practices across operational locations. Significant operational incidents and successful response strategies will be documented to strengthen institutional knowledge and improve future operational planning.

As EIB Stratoc continues to expand its operational footprint, particular attention will be given to organizational readiness for additional deployments. Findings from the expansion readiness assessment conducted during Phase II will be translated into practical recommendations covering manpower planning, supervisory capacity, operational infrastructure, communication systems, equipment requirements, and support services. These recommendations will support Executive Management in making informed decisions regarding future deployments while ensuring that organizational growth does not compromise operational quality or service delivery.

To further strengthen operational resilience, an Operational Improvement Register will be established within the Office of the Deputy General Manager. This register will capture recommendations arising from management reviews, operational inspections, staff engagements, report reviews, and field observations. Each recommendation will be assigned responsible officers, implementation timelines, and progress indicators to ensure continuous monitoring beyond the initial ninety-day implementation period. This mechanism will reinforce a culture of accountability while ensuring that operational improvements remain an ongoing organizational priority rather than a one-time exercise.

The organization will also conclude the implementation period with an Executive Operational Review involving the General Manager, Deputy General Manager, Camp Manager, Human Resource Officer, Team Leads, and other key operational stakeholders. The review will assess achievements recorded during the implementation period, evaluate progress against established Key Performance Indicators, identify outstanding operational challenges, and develop recommendations for the next phase of organizational improvement. The outcome of this review will serve as the foundation for future operational planning and strategic decision-making.

By the conclusion of the ninety-day implementation period, EIB Stratoc is expected to have established stronger operational governance, improved surveillance coordination, enhanced reporting standards, strengthened communication across all operational levels, improved personnel accountability, and reinforced management oversight. More importantly, the organization will have developed practical systems capable of sustaining continuous improvement while supporting future operational expansion and strengthening its reputation as a provider of professional intelligence-driven security solutions.

CONCLUSION

This 90-Day Strategic Operational Excellence Plan provides EIB Stratoc Ltd with a structured and practical roadmap for strengthening surveillance operations, enhancing organizational coordination, improving reporting quality, and reinforcing operational accountability.

The Plan recognizes the achievements already made by the organization, including the establishment of the Report Review and Analysis (RRA) Team, the introduction of standardized reporting processes, and the commencement of technical and responsibility-based refresher sessions and builds upon these initiatives to create a more integrated and sustainable operational framework.

Rather than introducing change for its own sake, this strategy focuses on strengthening existing systems, improving operational effectiveness, and addressing identified gaps through practical, measurable, and achievable initiatives. By reinforcing collaboration between Headquarters, Fusion Centres, operational camps, and supporting units, the organization will be better positioned to deliver timely intelligence, responsive operational support, and high-quality security services.

Successful implementation will require commitment from all levels of the organization. Through disciplined execution, regular performance monitoring, and continuous improvement, EIB Stratoc will enhance its operational readiness, support its planned expansion into new operational locations, and reinforce its position as a trusted provider of intelligence-driven security and surveillance solutions.
