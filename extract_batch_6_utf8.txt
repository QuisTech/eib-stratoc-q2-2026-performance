Human Resources Standard Operating Procedure (HR SOP) 
 
1. Purpose 
 
To establish standardized procedures for recruiting, onboarding, managing, developing, 
and separating employees while ensuring compliance with company policies and legal 
requirements and a professional human workflow. 
 
2. Scope 
 
This SOP applies to: 
 
➢ Human Resources Department 
➢ Executive Management 
➢ Senior Management 
➢ Department Heads 
➢ All Employees 
 
3. HR Responsibilities 
 
The HR Department is responsible for: 
 
✓ Workforce planning 
✓ Recruitment and selection 
✓ Employee onboarding 
✓ Personnel records management 
✓ Performance management 
✓ Employee relations 
✓ Employee promotion 
✓ Compensation and benefits administration 
✓ Learning and development 
✓ Attendance and leave management 
✓ Policy implementation 


Page 2:
✓ Disciplinary processes 
✓ Employee exit management 
 
 
 
 
SOP 1: Recruitment & Hiring 
 
Process 
 
1. Requesting Department Head submits a Manpower Requisition Form to the HR. 
2. Executive Management is notified and approves the request. 
3. HR prepares the job description and advertises the vacancy. 
4. HR screens applications. 
5. Qualified candidates are shortlisted. 
6. Interviews are conducted. 
7. Reference/background checks are completed (where applicable). 
8. Successful candidate receives an offer letter. 
9. Employment contract is signed. 
10. Employee file is created. 
 
Timeline: 2–4 weeks (depending on role and urgency)

SOP 12: Grievance Management 
 
Employees should: 
 
1. Raise concerns with their supervisor. 
2. Escalate unresolved issues to GM and HR. 
3. HR investigates impartially. 
4. HR recommends a resolution. 
5. Management approves where required. 
6. HR communicates the outcome.

An effective onboarding and offboarding process ensures consistency, protects 
company assets and information, and creates a positive employee experience. 
 
Staff Onboarding Process 
 
1. Pre-Employment 
 
* Issue the employment offer and receive signed acceptance. 
* Complete background/reference checks (if applicable). 
* Collect required documents: 
✓ Valid identification 
✓ Passport photograph 
✓ Bank details 
✓ Tax/Pension information (where applicable) 
✓ Next-of-kin details 
✓ P11 form 
✓ Guarantor form 
✓ Academic and professional certificates 
✓ Signed employment contract 
✓ Signed confidentiality/NDA agreement 
✓ Employee handbook acknowledgment 
✓ Etc  
 
2. Prepare for the Employee’s First Day 
 
* Create company email and user accounts. 
* Prepare workstation, laptop, ID card, uniform (where applicable), and access cards. 
* Inform relevant departments (HR, ICT, Finance, Security, Administration). 
* Assign a supervisor or mentor. 
* Develop a 30-, 60-, and 90-day onboarding plan. 
 
3. First Day Orientation 


Page 2:
 
* Welcome and introductions. 
* Explain the company’s: 
    * Vision and mission 
    * Organizational structure 
    * Policies and code of conduct 
    * Health and safety procedures 
    * Working hours and attendance expectations 
    * Leave policy 
    * Reporting lines 
    * Communication channels 
* Tour the office and introduce key team members. 
 
4. Role-Specific Induction 
 
* Review the job description and responsibilities. 
* Explain departmental processes and SOPs. 
* Set initial KPIs and performance expectations. 
* Provide necessary systems and operational training. 
 
5. Asset Handover 
 
Record and obtain signatures for all issued company assets, such as: 
 
* Laptop 
* Desktop 
* Mobile phone ( where applicable) 
* SIM card ( where applicable) 


Page 3:
* ID card 
* Keys 
* Vehicle (if applicable) 
* Uniform/PPE 
* Company credit card 
* Access cards 
 
6. Performance Review 
 
Conduct structured reviews after: 
 
* 30 days 
* 60 days 
* 90 days 
* End of probation 
 
Staff Offboarding Process 
 
1. Receive Exit Notification 
 
This may result from: 
 
* Resignation 
* Retirement 
* Contract expiration 
* Termination 
* Redundancy 
 


Page 4:
2. Notify Relevant Departments 
 
Inform: 
 
* HR 
* ICT 
* Finance 
* Security 
* Administration 
* Departmental Head 
 
3. Knowledge Transfer 
 
Require the employee to: 
 
* Document ongoing work. 
* Hand over projects. 
* Train the replacement where necessary. 
* Submit or change passwords only through approved company procedures if 
applicable. 
 
4. Recover Company Assets 
 
Collect and verify all issued assets: 
 
* Laptop 
* Phone 
* ID card 


Page 5:
* Access cards 
* Keys 
* Vehicle 
* Documents 
* Equipment 
* Uniforms/PPE 
 
Use an asset return checklist signed by both the employee and the receiving officer. 
 
5. Disable Access 
 
Coordinate with ICT to: 
 
* Disable email. 
* Remove system access. 
* Deactivate VPN and remote access. 
* Recover software licenses where applicable. 
* Disable building access. 
 
6. Financial Clearance 
 
Confirm: 
 
* Final salary 
* Outstanding reimbursements 
* Loan repayments (if any) 
* Leave balance 
* Pension and statutory deductions 


Page 6:
* Return of company funds or advances 
 
7. Exit Interview 
 
Discuss: 
 
* Reasons for leaving 
* Employee experience 
* Management feedback 
* Suggestions for improvement 
* Potential risks or unresolved issues 
 
8. Issue Clearance 
 
Ensure each department signs off that: 
 
* Assets have been returned. 
* Financial obligations are settled. 
* System access has been revoked. 
* Documentation is complete. 
 
9. Final Documentation 
 
Provide: 
 
* Acceptance of resignation (if applicable) 
* Final payslip 
* Employment/reference letter (where appropriate) 


Page 7:
* Tax and statutory documents 
* Exit acknowledgment 
 
Recommended Checklists 
 
Maintain standardized forms for: 
 
* New Employee Onboarding Checklist 
* Employee Orientation Checklist 
* Asset Issuance Form 
* Probation Evaluation Form 
* Exit Clearance Form 
* Asset Return Form 
* Exit Interview Form 
 
This structured process helps ensure new employees become productive quickly while 
sprotecting the organization during employee departures.
