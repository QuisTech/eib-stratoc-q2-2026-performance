90-DAY PROJECTION PLAN, STAFF REQUIREMENT, STRATEGIC 
BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT, INTERIOR & EXTERIOR 
DESIGN/FINISHING PLAN AND GROWTH STRATEGY 
 
Reporting Period: 1st July – 30th September 2026 
 
Prepared By: The General Manager 
 
 
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
 
This 90-day strategic plan is designed to position Briech Atlantic Ltd as a 
leading company in Construction, Estate Development, Estate Marketing, 
Interior and Exterior Design & Finishing Services. The plan focuses on 
strengthening the company's workforce, expanding business opportunities, 
improving operational efficiency, increasing revenue generation, and enhancing 
corporate visibility. 
 
2. CORE BUSINESS AREAS 
 
Briech Atlantic Ltd shall operate in the following areas: 
 
1. Construction and Civil Engineering Works. 
2. Estate Development and Property Development. 
3. Estate Marketing and Real Estate Consultancy. 
4. Interior Design and Space Planning. 
5. Exterior Design and Landscaping. 
6. Interior and Exterior Finishing Works. 
7. Building Renovation and Remodelling. 
8. Project Management and Facility Management. 
9. Procurement and Supply of Materials. 
10. General Contracting and Infrastructure Development.

9. CONCLUSION 
The period from 1st July to 30th September 2026 presents a strategic 
opportunity for Briech Atlantic Ltd to consolidate and expand its footprint in 
Construction, Estate Development, Estate Marketing, and Interior & 
Exterior Design and Finishing Services. 
By implementing aggressive business development initiatives, strategic 
recruitment, enhanced branding, and a culture of operational excellence, the 
company is well-positioned to achieve sustainable growth, increased 
profitability, improved customer satisfaction, and a stronger competitive edge 
within the Nigerian real estate and construction industry. 
The successful execution of this 90-Day Projection Plan will depend not only on 
recruiting competent personnel but also on providing the necessary operational 
support systems, including adequate logistics, dedicated mobility, modern 
technology, and efficient work tools. 
In particular, the provision of dedicated vehicles for the Marketing and Business 
Development Team, investment in robust ICT infrastructure, acquisition of 
high-performance desktop systems for Architects and Engineers, strengthened 
branding initiatives, and enhanced support for the Interior and Exterior Design 
Unit will significantly improve productivity, accelerate project delivery, 
increase project acquisition, and drive overall business growth. 
With these strategic investments and initiatives in place, Briech Atlantic Ltd 
will be better positioned to achieve its corporate objectives, increase 
profitability, enhance operational efficiency, and reinforce its reputation as a 
leading construction, estate development, and finishing company in Nigeria.

AN ALREADY EXISTING PUBLIC SAFETY 
ANSWERING POINT (PSAP) 
Background 
 
The Public Safety Answering Point (PSAP) serves 
as the first point of contact for individuals seeking 
emergency assistance. It plays a significant role in 
receiving distress calls, gathering accurate 
information, and coordinating timely responses 
from emergency services such as police, fire and 
medical teams. 
 
The Public Safety Answering Point (PSAP) facility 
has already been established, with the physical 
infrastructure and core technology in place. 
However, it is not yet fully operational due to the 
absence of a functional Call Centre and 
incomplete integration with emergency response 
agencies. 
This 90-day strategy focuses on transforming the 
existing PSAP into a fully operational, technology-
enabled emergency communication and dispatch

Strategic Objectives 
● Establish a fully functional Emergency Call 
Centre. 
● Operationalize the PSAP on a 24/7 basis. 
● Integrate all emergency and security agencies 
into one coordinated response platform. 
● Deploy and optimize PSAP software solutions 
such as the PSAP application, COMMAND 
OPS and CIGAS 
● Recruit and train competent personnel. 
● Improve on the already existing Standard 
Operating Procedures (SOPs). 
● Improve emergency response coordination 
and public safety. 
● Launch public awareness and stakeholder 
engagement initiatives.
